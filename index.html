Plumbing emergency in Hudson? Do this first

Water that is still moving does the most damage, so stopping it comes before anything else. The same steps apply to a burst pipe, a failed water heater and a sump pump that quit in a storm.

  • Close the main shutoff, usually in the basement near the water meter, by turning it clockwise.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • Move valuables and rugs out of the wet area, and clear a path to the shutoff and the water heater.
  • Call (508) 389-4045. If sewage is coming up, stop using every fixture and keep children and pets out.

Hudson plumbing, from downtown Main Street to the Assabet River

Hudson sits in Middlesex County along the Assabet River, and its housing runs from older downtown blocks and mill-era streets to newer subdivisions. Feltonville is the former village name for what is now Downtown Hudson around Main Street. Older pipe, wherever it is found, fails in familiar ways, such as rust in galvanized supply lines, scaled cast iron drains and clay laterals full of roots.

Buildings close to the river and on low streets can have basements near groundwater, so a sump pump matters there. The Fort Meadow Reservoir area is shared with Marlborough, and Lake Boon and Gleasondale sit on the Stow line, where many homes have private wells and septic systems.

Interstate 495 Exit 26 is about half a mile from downtown, which is how dispatch reaches Marlborough, Sudbury and the other towns we serve. According to the Department of Public Works, the Town runs its own sewer system with about 51 miles of gravity sewer, and it has its own electric utility, Hudson Light and Power. We work with the Building Department at 78 Main Street on permits.

What we handle, and when to pick which service

Most emergency calls start with one of four problems. A drain that will not clear belongs with drain cleaning, and a main line that backs up the whole house belongs with sewer line cleaning. A heater that has quit goes to water heater repair or, if the tank is leaking, water heater replacement.

Water where it should not be is the fourth. A spraying pipe is burst pipe repair, and a stain with no visible source is leak detection. Planned work is just as welcome. Repiping, installation and commercial plumbing are scheduled around your calendar.
