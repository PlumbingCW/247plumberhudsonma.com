Emergency Water Service Line Repair in Hudson, MA

The service line carries water from the street to your house. When it leaks, the meter runs, pressure drops and a patch of lawn stays wet. We find the break and fix it the right way.

Signs the buried line has failed

Water service line repair in Hudson, MA is urgent when the supply to the house is lost or flooding the yard. Our line is open 24/7 and we offer same-day appointments when available. These signs point to a problem between the street and the house.

  • The meter keeps turning after you close the main valve inside the house.
  • A soft, green or muddy patch appears in the lawn or driveway, even in a dry week.
  • Water pressure drops suddenly, or the house has no water at all.
  • You hear running water with every fixture closed.

Who owns what in Hudson

The Town of Hudson DPW maintains the public water main. The service pipe from the street to your house belongs to the homeowner, and the Town states that service work must be done by a contractor on its approved Water and Sewer Contractor list. That list is kept by the DPW, so confirm your contractor is on it before anyone digs.

Call 811 before digging. The Town can tell you where the main and the curb stop are, and your plumber will mark the line before any trench opens.

Hudson's drinking water is a blend of surface water from the Gates Pond Reservoir and groundwater from town wells. The Town treats it at its own facilities before it reaches the main in your street.

Finding the leak without trenching the yard

We start by proving the leak is on your side of the meter. A pressure test or a meter check separates the house from the line. Then we locate the break with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, so the excavation is a small hole, not a long trench.

Choosing between a spot repair and a full replacement depends on age, material and how many breaks the line has had.

Low water pressure

Low pressure does not always mean a leak. It can be a partly closed valve, a clogged filter, a failing pressure reducing valve at the house or a service line choked with mineral buildup. We check the easy things first.

If pressure drops at every tap at once, the service line or the meter is the likely cause. If it drops at one fixture only, the cause is local to that fixture. See faucet repair for that case.

Meter pit, curb stop and the repair in the street

Before anyone touches the curb stop or the meter pit, ask the DPW who owns each one. The Town maintains the main, and your plumber needs to know exactly where the Town's part ends and yours begins. A leak on the house side of that line is yours to fix.

The Town's water department says backflow preventers on commercial buildings and irrigation systems are installed and inspected under its program, and installers must be licensed through the DPW. If you have an irrigation system, ask us when its device was last tested.

Rules for the service line

A water service line repair follows the standard in the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00 for pipe material, depth and connections, and needs a permit under 248 CMR 3.05. Call 811 before digging.

The Town of Hudson DPW runs the public water system. Call it at 978-562-9333 if you are not sure where the Town's part of the line ends and yours begins.
