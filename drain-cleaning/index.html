When a clogged drain is an emergency

Drain cleaning in Hudson, MA means removing a blockage from a fixture drain or the main line, then working out why it formed. A single slow sink can wait for a daytime visit. Water coming up in a tub, a toilet that overflows when the washer drains, or sewage smell in the basement cannot wait. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, nights, weekends and holidays included.

Here is what to do while you wait for the plumber:

  • Stop using sinks, showers, toilets, the dishwasher and the washing machine. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere.
  • Keep chemical drain cleaner in the cabinet. It can sit in a standing blockage and splash back on the plumber.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from floor drains and the lowest bathroom.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. Tell dispatch whether it is one drain or all of them.

What a clogged drain in Hudson usually means

Hudson grew as a shoe-manufacturing mill town, and the older streets around downtown and Feltonville still hold homes that predate modern plumbing code. Cast iron and clay drain lines in those houses scale and roughen inside over the decades. Hair, soap and grease catch on that rough surface and build up into a plug.

The pattern tells you a lot. If one sink or tub is slow, the blockage sits in that fixture's branch line. If the toilet, the shower and the kitchen sink all gurgle together, the problem is further down in the main drain. A main-line blockage needs a heavier machine than a hand snake.

The Town of Hudson says plainly that nothing but sewage belongs in the sewer pipes. Wipes sold as flushable, cooking grease and paper towels are the usual culprits we pull out. The Town's sewer crew clears stoppages in its own collection system, so if your plumber finds the main street line is the problem, the DPW is the next call.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathrooms. Grease cools in the pipe and coats the walls, then coffee grounds and food scraps stick to it. The sink drains a little slower each month until it stops.

We cable the line past the trap and into the wall, then flush it with hot water to see how it runs. If a garbage disposal sits in the way, we check that it is not the real obstruction. A disposal that hums but will not spin is a different job, covered on the garbage disposal repair page.

Slow tubs, showers and bathroom sinks

A bathroom drain that is slow but not blocked is almost always hair and soap wrapped around the stopper or the first few inches of pipe. Pull the stopper and clean it before you call. If water still pools, the buildup is deeper and a short cable run clears it.

When a slow drain returns within weeks of being cleared, stop treating the symptom. A drain line leak or a sagging section of pipe can hold water and catch debris, and a camera shows it quickly. Leaks inside a wall are a separate job for leak detection.

Rules that touch a drain job

Clearing a stoppage is exempt from the permit requirement in the state plumbing and gas regulations, 248 CMR 3.05. Replacing or altering drain pipe is not exempt, and the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00, sets the standard for traps, slopes and vents that a replacement must meet.

In Hudson, the Department of Public Works says only sewage belongs in the sewer. That is the reason we ask what went down the drain before we choose a tool.
