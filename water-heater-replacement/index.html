A failed tank is an emergency

Water heater replacement in Hudson, MA is urgent when the tank leaks from the body, the floor is flooding, or the unit is more than a decade old and has stopped working. Dispatch is 24/7. We ask a few questions on the phone so the plumber arrives with the right unit.

  • Close the cold water supply valve above the tank.
  • Turn off the electric breaker or the gas control before any water reaches wiring or the burner.
  • Photograph the leak and the nameplate on the tank. The nameplate shows age, size and fuel type.
  • Soak up standing water with towels, and open a basement window if the room is damp.

Repair or replace: the decision, plainly

A tank wears out from the inside. Once water seeps through the steel, no repair stops it. Replacement is the answer when the tank body leaks, when it is past its expected life and has a second fault, or when the repair costs more than a third of a new unit.

If the unit is only a few years old and the fault is a thermostat or element, a repair is cheaper. We tell you which case you are in. See water heater repair for that route.

What the day looks like

The plumber shuts off water and power, drains the old tank, disconnects it and carries it out. The new unit is set in the same spot when the layout allows, connected with new supply lines, and fitted with a pressure and temperature relief valve piped to a safe drain.

We check the gas line, venting and combustion air on gas units. We confirm the expansion tank is sized for the house. A closed system with no expansion tank can push pressure back through the plumbing. Installation details live on the water heater installation page.

Permits and who is allowed to do the work

In Massachusetts, only a licensed master or journeyman plumber can replace a water heater, and permits are issued only to licensed plumbers. Hudson's Building Department is at 78 Main Street and has plumbing and gas inspectors. Ask any plumber to show you the permit for the job.

The Massachusetts board that licenses plumbers lets you check a license by phone or online. If a price sounds too good, check the license before you sign.

The standard a replacement must meet

A replacement tank must meet the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00 for relief valve piping and drain pans where needed, and a gas unit must meet the Massachusetts Fuel Gas Code, 248 CMR 4.00. Both need permits under 248 CMR 3.05, with emergency provisions for urgent installs.

Massachusetts also requires carbon monoxide alarms in homes with fuel burning appliances, which is why we check yours at a gas water heater install.
