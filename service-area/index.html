Plumbing Service Area Around Hudson, MA

Dispatch is based in Hudson and covers five nearby towns, each with its own page. Call any hour and we confirm your address.

Call (508) 389-4045

The five towns with their own pages

Each page has local details, an emergency checklist and a ZIP coverage answer for that town.

Marlborough, MA 01752

Marlborough is the closest large market, about four miles south of Hudson. Its mix of older neighborhoods and newer condominium complexes means calls range from root-choked clay laterals to failed water heaters in shared buildings. See emergency plumber in Marlborough for the city's after-hours water and sewer contact.

Stow, MA 01775

Stow lies north of Hudson and shares Gleasondale and Lake Boon with it. Many homes use private wells and septic systems, so dispatch asks about both. The Stow page explains what we fix on well and septic properties and what needs a specialist.

Sudbury, MA 01776

Sudbury sits east of Hudson. Larger houses, finished basements and long pipe runs raise the cost of a hidden leak, which is why the Sudbury page leans on leak location, repiping and sump pump backups.

Maynard, MA 01754

Maynard is the mill town northeast of Hudson along the Assabet River, with houses built for the Assabet Woolen Mill workforce. The Maynard page covers what old galvanized, cast iron and clay pipe do and what to replace.

Berlin, MA 01503

Berlin is the rural neighbor to the west. Roads are long and night calls are hard to place, so the Berlin page covers private wells, free well testing through the Town and how to help dispatch find your door.

How we choose the towns

Dispatch is in Hudson, so the nearest towns are the ones we reach fastest. Marlborough borders Hudson on the south, while Stow and Berlin sit on other edges of town. Sudbury and Maynard are a short drive over local roads. Each town was added because it has its own housing, utilities and plumbing problems, not because it was easy to list.

Hudson itself is covered on every service page. A good place to start is drain cleaning or water heater repair, which are the most common calls from town addresses. For a burst pipe, go straight to burst pipe repair.

Nearby places we can often reach

Beyond the five towns, we can often reach Southborough and Hopkinton by way of Marlborough, Clinton by way of Berlin and Boxborough by way of Stow or Maynard. Bolton is also close to Berlin and Hudson. We do not give these places a page of their own, since we would have little to say about them that is real and specific.

Call and give dispatch your address. Arrival depends on the schedule, the weather and the roads, so dispatch gives an honest estimate, not a promise. If an address is too far for a safe emergency response, we say so, and you can call someone closer.

You can call (508) 389-4045 to confirm coverage before a problem starts. It takes a minute, and it means you know who to ring at two in the morning.

What a good coverage answer sounds like

When you call, dispatch asks for your street, the nearest cross street and the town. We check where the nearest technician is and give an arrival window. If you are on the edge of the area, we tell you before you wait, and we say so even when the answer is a longer drive.

Please do not wait for us if there is danger. Call 911 for fire, a gas smell or an electrical hazard, and call us for the plumbing once everyone is safe.

Local rules differ by town

Each town has its own utilities and rules, and according to their own published pages those change what a plumber does. Hudson has its own sewer system and its own electric department. Marlborough runs a city water and sewer division with a separate after-hours number. Stow and Berlin lean on private wells, so their Boards of Health set the rules for wells and septic systems.

Sump pumps are a good example. Hudson does not allow them to discharge into the sanitary sewer, and Marlborough runs a program against illegal sump pump and roof drain connections. The right fix is the same in both, a discharge to the yard or a dry well, but the person you call about it is different. Each town page names the right office.

Why the five towns get their own pages

A page earns its place only if it can say something real about the town. Marlborough has a city sewer division and an after-hours contact. Stow and Berlin have private well rules and, in Berlin, a free well testing program. Maynard has mill-era housing and an Assabet River basement problem. Sudbury has larger homes and a town wastewater plan.

Places that could not support that kind of detail, such as Bolton or Boxborough, are mentioned on the nearest page instead of getting a thin one.
