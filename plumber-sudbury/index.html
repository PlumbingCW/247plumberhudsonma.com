Why a leak costs more in a bigger house

Emergency plumbing in Sudbury, MA often involves larger homes with finished basements, second-floor laundry rooms and several bathrooms. A leak in one of those houses can run a long time before anyone notices, and the damage reaches finished drywall, flooring and mechanical rooms. Dispatch is open 24/7 and Sudbury is about seven miles east of Hudson.

The first thing to do is stop the water. After that, locating the source quickly is what saves the most money. See leak detection for how we find hidden leaks.

Before we arrive

These steps help while the technician is on the way.

Shut off the water

Find the main valve where the line enters the house and turn it clockwise. If you cannot find it, close the nearest fixture valve and tell dispatch.

Kill power near water

Turn off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely. Never step into standing water near outlets.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet zone. Open a path to the water heater, the cleanout and the main shutoff.

Call and describe it

Call (508) 389-4045 and tell dispatch your Sudbury address, where the water is coming from and whether sewage is involved.

Water and wastewater in Sudbury

According to the Town, Sudbury operates municipal water supply wells on Raymond Road, and it has an ongoing comprehensive wastewater management plan that reviews septic systems, including those in the Route 20 business district. Sudbury also lists three small local treatment facilities, at Avalon, Longfellow Glen and the Lincoln-Sudbury High School.

In practice, many Sudbury homes use on-site septic. If you have a backup, tell dispatch which kind of system you have. A blockage in the house line is ours to clear. A full tank is for a septic hauler, and the Town and its Board of Health are the right contacts for rules.

Older colonial and mid-century houses

Sudbury has houses from many eras. Older colonials may have additions with mixed pipe materials, and mid-century homes may have copper that is now past middle age.

If you have had two or more pinhole leaks, ask about replacing the weak section rather than patching each leak. We explain the choice and the cost before any work begins. See repiping.

Sump pumps and basements in storms

Finished basements in Sudbury depend on sump pumps in heavy rain. If the power goes out in a storm, the pump stops. A battery backup protects the space. Test your pump before spring and fall by pouring water into the pit.

If the pump fails and water rises, call right away. See sump pump repair.

What a visit looks like

When you call, dispatch asks where the water is, whether it is still running and whether sewage is involved. We give an arrival window and stay on the phone if you need help finding the shutoff. The technician starts by stopping the water and making the space safe, then finds the cause before quoting the repair.

You see the price before work begins. If the job needs a permit, such as a water heater swap or a new line, we pull it through the town. For a larger house with several zones of plumbing, we label shutoffs as we work so the next repair is faster.
