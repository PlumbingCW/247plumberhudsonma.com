Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Hudson 24/7 Plumbing, based at 2 Coolidge St, Hudson, MA 01749, handles information when you visit this website, call us or email us.

Phone calls and recordings

Calls to (508) 389-4045 may be recorded for quality and training purposes. A recording can include your voice, your phone number, your address and the details of the plumbing problem you describe. We use recordings to dispatch correctly, to check the quality of service and to train staff. Tell the caller at the start if you do not want to be recorded, and we will do our best to help another way.

Information from this website

This website has no contact forms, accounts or checkout, so it does not collect personal details through the pages themselves. Your browser sends standard technical data to our host, such as your IP address and the pages you request, as with any website. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page first.

Email

If you email contact@247plumberhudsonma.com, we receive your address and anything you write. We use it to answer you and keep it for as long as we need it to serve you.

How we use and share information

We use what you give us to schedule and perform plumbing work, to send an arrival notice or a receipt and to meet legal and permit duties, such as a permit application filed with the Town. We do not sell personal information. We may share it with a subcontractor who is working on your job, with a payment processor and with the Town where a permit requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep. Write to the email address above or call us.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Coverage

Our service area around Hudson, MA

Dispatch covers Hudson and the nearby towns of Marlborough, Stow, Sudbury, Maynard and Berlin. The map is centered on Hudson, so you can see which direction each town sits.
