Burst pipe: first five minutes

Burst pipe repair in Hudson, MA begins before the plumber arrives. Water from a split line will keep running until the supply is closed. Our line is staffed 24/7 and we offer same-day appointments when available. Here is the order that protects your house.

  • Find the main shutoff, usually where the water line enters the basement, and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  • Open the lowest faucet and the highest faucet to drain the pipes.
  • Switch off the breaker to any room with standing water. Do not step in water near outlets.
  • Move belongings, lift rugs and start removing water with towels or a wet vacuum.
  • Call us, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Why pipes burst in Hudson

New England winters are the main cause. Water expands as it freezes and the pressure splits the pipe, usually at a fitting or a weak spot, not where the ice is. The break often shows up when the ice thaws and water starts flowing again.

The places that freeze first are the ones nobody heats. Hudson homes with water lines in an exterior wall, a crawlspace, an attached garage or a bulkhead are the usual victims. Older houses near downtown and in Feltonville often have pipes added over many decades, which means mixed materials and joints in odd places.

Not every burst is cold weather. Pinholes in old copper, a failed compression fitting, a corroded galvanized elbow and a faulty washing machine hose all cause the same flood. We find the actual cause and repair that.

Frozen but not burst yet

If a pipe is frozen but not split, you still have time. Keep the faucet open so water can move once the ice melts. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or a heating pad. Never use an open flame.

If you cannot find the frozen section or you hear running water behind the wall, close the main and call. Thawing a pipe that has already cracked turns a small problem into a flood.

Cracked drain pipes and broken supply valves

A cracked drain line leaks only when something drains, so the damage is slow and smelly. A broken supply valve under a sink or behind a toilet can fail all at once. We stop the water and replace the valve, often on the same visit. Fixture valves are also covered on the faucet and sink repair page.

After the repair: drying and insurance

We repair the pipe and test the line for leaks under pressure. Water that has soaked into floors and walls needs drying fast. If wet drywall or carpet sits for more than a day or two, mold starts. We can tell you what a restoration company will need to see.

A burst pipe is a sudden event, so many homeowners policies cover the resulting water damage. Coverage for the pipe itself varies. Keep receipts and photos, and ask your insurer before you throw anything away.

Emergency work and the permit rule

The state regulation, 248 CMR 3.05, has a provision for emergencies. A licensed plumber may repair or temporarily restore a fixture before the permit is issued if the work has been tested and the inspector is notified. A regular inspection must follow no later than the next working day.

In plain terms, a burst pipe does not wait for paperwork, and the permit follows. Pipe that is replaced must meet the standard in the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00.
